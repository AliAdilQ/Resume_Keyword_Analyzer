A LITTLE CLARITY GOES A LONG WAY

Built to make your experience visible.

Resume Keyword Analyzer is an open-source project by AliAdilQ.
It helps you compare the language in your resume with the role you're pursuing.

How the analysis works

  1. Extract the text. pypdf reads text-based PDFs, python-docx reads paragraphs and tables, and TXT files are decoded as UTF-8. Scanned PDFs require OCR outside this app.
  2. Normalize the language. NLTK tokenization, spaCy stopwords, and lookup lemmatization clean English text. Technical names and multiword skills are protected.
  3. Find the important terms. A categorized skill dictionary detects technologies. Single-document TF-IDF ranks additional terms, including recurring bigrams, for each document.
  4. Compare the documents. Weighted job-keyword coverage, pair-fitted unigram/bigram TF-IDF cosine similarity, and known skill overlap produce the report.
  5. Offer practical suggestions. Rules surface gaps, missing skills sections, short resumes, and opportunities to describe measurable impact.

Use the score thoughtfully

Text similarity reflects shared wording. It does not understand your full experience, assess your qualifications, or predict a hiring decision. It is not a certified ATS score, an LLM, or an embedding-based semantic matcher.

Add keywords only when they accurately describe your experience. A clear, credible resume matters more than maximizing a number.

THE SCORING FORMULA

Transparent by default

Keyword coverageTechnical skills count twice. Repeated job terms receive a capped boost.

TF-IDF similarityCosine similarity measures overlap in weighted words and phrases.
20%
Skill overlapFraction of recognized job skills present in the resume.

If the job contains no recognized skills, keyword coverage receives 70% and similarity receives 30%.

Privacy, explained

Original files are processed in memory and discarded. Extracted resume text, the job description, and results are stored in SQLite for your history.

You and authorized administrators can access these records. Deleting an analysis removes its stored text and result.
